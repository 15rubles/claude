How to play

MouseYour queen follows the cursor
Eat green crumbs — every 3 hatch a new worker
Left clickWorkers charge the cursor for 2 seconds (your queen is left unguarded!)
GuardYour workers form rings around your queen to protect her
ConquerIf any of your ants touches an enemy queen, her colony is yours
PickupsFire Chili makes your ants fight harder · Sugar Rush speeds your queen and readies your charge
PlacesHold a Fungus Garden alone to farm ants · Tunnels carry your colony to the linked hole
MToggle music · Esc opens options
BewareOne enemy ant reaching your queen ends your reign
Tip: stand still to brace your guards. Charge through a guard ring to reach the queen inside. Size tags are green when your colony is bigger, red when it is smaller.
