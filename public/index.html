How to play

Controls
MouseMove your queen
EscPause & options
Left clickorSpaceCharge
MMusic on / off
TouchHold a finger to steer, lift it to brace. Tap CHARGE (or two fingers) to attack.
EnterStart / restart
Rules
Grow - eat green crumbs; every 3 hatch a new worker.
Guard - workers ring your queen. Stand still to brace them.
Charge - workers rush the cursor for 2s, leaving your queen unguarded.
Conquer - any of your ants touching an enemy queen takes her whole colony.
Beware - one enemy ant reaching your queen ends your reign.
Size tags - green: smaller than you, red: bigger. Small colonies move faster.
Streaks - conquer again within 6s for bonus ants.
Power-ups
Fire Chili - your ants fight much harder for 10s.
Sugar Rush - faster queen for 8s and your charge is ready.
The map
Fungus Garden - stand inside alone to farm new ants.
Tunnels - step in to move your whole colony to the linked hole.
Rocks block your path, puddles slow you down.
Events - storms, picnics, earthquakes and mating flights shake things up.
Play with friends
Pick a room name, press Copy invite link and send it. Everyone who opens the link plays in the same world. Other players show with a ★ on the leaderboard.
